About

The Artist

Deb Mortl is a painter and jewelry artist whose work is rooted in the rhythms of the coast — the way light breaks across water, the weight of wet sand, the slow erosion of stone.

Her paintings are layered explorations of texture and abstraction, built up with palette knives, brushes, and found materials. Each piece holds the memory of a specific place, a specific tide.

Her jewelry extends the same sensibility into wearable form — organic shapes cast in silver and bronze, finished with the same attention to surface and touch that defines her canvases.

Deb works from her studio on the coast, where the light changes everything.
